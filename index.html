08 // Expedition Briefing

FIELD Notes & FAQ

Everything you need to know about gear, beginner readiness, safety protocols, custom routing, and weather flexibility before clipping in.

What should I pack for an adventure trip?
We send a terrain-specific packing blueprint 60 days before departure. As a baseline, pack moisture-wicking merino base layers, a windproof hardshell jacket, broken-in waterproof trekking boots, UV-400 sunglasses, a reusable insulated bottle, and a 30L daypack. All technical safety hardware — harnesses, helmets, crampons, wetsuits, tents, and satellite beacons — is supplied and inspected by Meridian.
Are adventure activities suitable for beginners?
Absolutely. Over half of our guests are attempting their first high-altitude trek, tandem paraglide, or Class-III/IV whitewater descent. Every departure clearly lists its difficulty tier, and our guides hold unhurried ground-school briefings before launching so beginners feel confident while seasoned explorers still get genuine terrain thrill.
Are professional guides included?
Yes — 100% of Meridian expeditions include certified lead and assistant guides tailored to the sport (IFMGA/NIM mountaineers, IRF river guides, PADI Divemasters, and USHPA/APPI tandem pilots), maintaining a strict 1:4 guide-to-explorer ratio on technical days.
What safety measures do you follow?
Safety governs every mile. Every expedition carries dual Garmin inReach satellite communicators, pulse oximeters and Gamow/oxygen protocols above 3,800m, wilderness first-responder (WFR) certified leads, daily aviation-grade weather windows, and pre-verified helicopter evacuation insurance coordination.
Can I customize my adventure package?
Yes. Any departure can be converted into a private custom expedition for solo travelers, couples, or friend groups. You can combine regions (such as Ladakh trekking followed by Rishikesh rafting), adjust daily elevation gains, upgrade lodges, or add dedicated expedition photography.
What happens if weather conditions change?
Mountain and ocean weather demands respect. Every Meridian itinerary builds in contingency buffer windows and pre-scouted alternate routes of equal beauty. If storms halt a flight or summit push, your lead guide seamlessly pivots to backup ridges, canyon explorations, or cultural immersions at zero extra surcharge.
What is your cancellation policy?
Plans evolve, so our terms stay fair and transparent: free date transfers or 100% expedition credit up to 30 days before departure, a 75% cash refund between 30 and 15 days out, and seamless travel-insurance documentation if unforeseen medical or flight disruptions occur inside 14 days.
